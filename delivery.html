Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

As a provider of purely digital IT services, software development, and consulting, no physical products are shipped.

1. Digital Delivery Protocol

All completed deliverables (source code, application builds, accessibility audit reports, and documentation) are delivered entirely digitally. Deliverables will be transferred to the client via secure Git repository transfer (e.g., GitHub, GitLab), encrypted ZIP files, or direct server deployment as dictated by the project requirements.

2. Delivery Timelines

Delivery timelines vary based on project complexity and scope. Exact delivery schedules and milestone deadlines are established and agreed upon in writing before project kickoff.

3. Confirmation of Delivery

Delivery is considered complete once the digital assets have been successfully transferred to the client's control or deployed to the agreed-upon testing/production environment.
