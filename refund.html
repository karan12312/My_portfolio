Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Due to the custom nature of software engineering, consulting, and development services, the following refund and cancellation terms apply to all engagements:

1. Non-Refundable Services

Once development, coding, or auditing work has commenced on an agreed-upon milestone, the payment for that specific milestone is strictly non-refundable. This is because time, labor, and technical expertise cannot be returned once expended.

2. Cancellations

Clients reserve the right to cancel a project at any time. If a project is cancelled, the client will only be billed for the work completed up to the date of cancellation. Any uncommenced future milestones will not be billed.

3. Advance Payments

If an advance payment was made for a project that is cancelled by the client before any work has begun, a full refund (minus any standard gateway processing fees) will be initiated within 5-7 business days.

4. Delivery Rejections

I offer revision cycles during the handover phase to ensure the software meets the agreed-upon technical specifications. Outright refunds are not provided for completed software that meets the original contractual requirements.
